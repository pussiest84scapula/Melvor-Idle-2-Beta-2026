## 🕹️ About

Melvor Idle 2 is a single-player idle RPG from Games by Malcs, expanding the original adventure with deeper skills, strategic combat, quests, offline progression, and a growing fantasy world. Train interconnected skills, manage resources, battle monsters and bosses, and uncover Melvor’s history at your own pace. Join the pre-release beta testing program, help shape development through feedback, and receive a free game key.

Melvor Idle 2 is for players who enjoy relaxing yet deep RPG progression, strategic planning, resource management, and adventures that fit around a busy schedule.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- ⚔️ **Strategic Combat** — Battle monsters, bosses, and dungeons with refined combat mechanics.
- 📈 **Deep Skill Progression** — Master 29 interconnected skills with meaningful progression and synergies.
- 📖 **Story-Driven Quests** — Explore Melvor’s history through quests, unique content, and powerful quest bosses.
- ⏳ **Offline Progression** — Continue gathering resources and advancing while you are away.
- 🌦️ **Dynamic World** — Experience customizable day-night cycles and changing weather.
- 🧰 **Improved Management** — Organize equipment, items, banks, tags, and loadouts with upgraded tools.
- 🧩 **Fully Moddable** — Customize and expand your adventure with user-created mods.
- 🤝 **Community Development** — Help guide the game through hands-on testing, feedback, surveys, and discussion.

**Q: What is the reward for testing?**
